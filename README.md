## Hi, I'm Pedro

I run Starmountain Flash from Covilhã, Portugal. I build my own tools
and I build websites and AI automations for Portuguese SMEs.

### What I build for myself

- **[Foco](https://github.com/pedromsilvape/foco)**: the CRM I use to run
  the business. Contacts, pipeline, projects, calendar and finances in one
  place, with a Telegram bot that sends me a daily summary.
  Next.js, Supabase, Stripe, Netlify.
- **StarMap**: the platform behind my automation diagnosis. A client's team
  logs their tasks for a week and I map where the time goes and what is
  worth automating. Next.js, Supabase, Netlify.
- **[StudyOS](https://gestaocrew.pt)**: a study platform for Management
  students at Universidade Aberta, where I am also a student.

### What I build for clients
